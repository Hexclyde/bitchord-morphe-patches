Bitchord+ install guide

Bitchord+ is BitChord with my patches added. The main patch plays music from your own Telegram channels, in the quality it was uploaded in. Others add things like pitch control, headphone correction and a DJ mode. You build Bitchord+ yourself, on your own phone, with Morphe Manager. This guide walks you through it on a pretend phone. Pick a tour, then tap the highlighted parts to go step by step. It’s written for patches version 1.1.0-beta.12 and BitChord 1.9. This installation guide illustrates setup; the combined build also includes the Downloads hub, batch source selection, public sharing and Telegram offloading.

Build Bitchord+ on your phone with Morphe Manager, then set up its extras. Pick a tour and tap the highlighted parts to follow along.
