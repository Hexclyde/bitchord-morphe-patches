DJM-Rec for Android
-------------------
https://github.com/P2GR/DJM-Rec-for-Android
Revision: 2f36d11b837f5d627740c77f3acc9b130c24b04f
Files: app/src/main/cpp/MeterCalculator.h, WaveformAnalyzer.cpp and WaveformAnalyzer.h
Adaptation: TransitionWaveform.java second-order Butterworth filter bank and
independent channel peak extraction, with timestamped bounded display bins.
The upstream recorder ring and fitted display curves are not included.
DeckMeter.java stereo peak/RMS calculations, -60 dBFS display
floor and 0.966 clipping threshold, with bounded UI hold/expiry. Independent
deck measurements are taken before the faders, not at the mixed output.
