## Fetched or used at run time, not bundled

- **Headphone correction** downloads headphone profiles from [AutoEq](https://github.com/jaakkopasanen/AutoEq) by Jaakko Pasanen ([MIT License](https://github.com/jaakkopasanen/AutoEq/blob/master/LICENSE)) when you pick a model, straight from its GitHub repository, and keeps them on the phone. The filter formulas are the peaking and shelving biquads of Robert Bristow-Johnson's Audio EQ Cookbook.
- The **JamesDSP** patch works with [RootlessJamesDSP](https://github.com/timschneeb/RootlessJamesDSP) by Tim Schneeberger and [JamesDSPManager](https://github.com/james34602/JamesDSPManager) by James Fung (both GPL-3.0), installed separately by you. Its setup help links to [Shizuku](https://github.com/RikkaApps/Shizuku) by RikkaApps (Apache-2.0), also installed separately, and its power switch is RootlessJamesDSP's own `SET_POWER_STATE` broadcast. Nothing of theirs is bundled; BitChord's audio session is announced to them through Android's standard audio-effect broadcasts.

# Streamseek design reference

The optional Streamseek previews feature was informed by ndr-brt/streamseek, commit `851efcfcbf66e8b3216191d56616af378e9573b0`, published under GPL-3.0. The Android implementation is independent; it bundles no Streamseek source, Node/Electron runtime or server. Upstream: https://github.com/ndr-brt/streamseek.

## SongMirror native import adaptation

The portable playlist parser conventions and weighted metadata confidence are adapted from [SongMirror](https://github.com/ahnafnafee/songmirror), revision `76d3d27801a786150832c414777c5646803e30e5`. This Android implementation uses BitChord's anonymous catalog search and native recording matcher. It does not bundle the Python server or reproduce every provider integration.

# Streamseek audio decoder

The optional Streamseek decoder uses FFmpeg 7.1.5 (libavformat, libavcodec,
libswresample and libavutil), licensed under LGPL-2.1-or-later. No GPL-only,
nonfree or network components are enabled. The exact unmodified upstream source
archive is included in `native/preview/vendor/ffmpeg-7.1.5.tar.xz`, with the
JNI adapter and build instructions alongside it. See
[native notices](native/preview/NOTICE) and [preview behavior](docs/STREAMSEEK-PREVIEW.md).
Upstream: https://ffmpeg.org/ .
