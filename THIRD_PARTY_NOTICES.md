The Telegram native libraries are shipped unchanged. They were built from these source commits:

- TGX-Android/tdlib: [`7ec241d`](https://github.com/TGX-Android/tdlib/tree/7ec241d026ee3563e615c25a700117c2abbaea19)
- tdlib/td: [`ea3f69c`](https://github.com/tdlib/td/tree/ea3f69ce11aef458bfbb09869b809ede4447486f)
- OpenSSL: [`45e844f`](https://github.com/openssl/openssl/tree/45e844fa2a14ec92d146bd8f5778ac130b6625fb)

The experimental Soulseek JNI library is built from this repository's
`native/soulseek` adapter and the vendored engine at
`d20e433a8979068acd80f4d4242125beaf4825ee`, with local embedded-mode,
resource-bound and Android compatibility changes recorded alongside its source.
Its Rust dependency graph is locked in `native/soulseek/Cargo.lock`; the
[dependency and Rust standard-library notices](licenses/soulseek-native-dependencies.txt)
are also copied into the patched APK.
The inventory covers the full lock graph, including build-time and non-Android
dependencies, and is generated by `tools/native_licenses.py`. It preserves the
Rust standard-library notice appendix and includes pinned upstream license texts
where published crates omit their workspace's license files.

SeekerAndroid and StreamSeek were inspected as design references; their apps,
.NET/Node runtimes and source code are not bundled in the Soulseek extension.

The download extension also bundles these independent, on-phone runtimes:

| Component | Used for | Source and notices |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PurpleI2P i2pd, Boost and OpenSSL | I2P download streams | Pinned inputs and build instructions in [native/i2p](native/i2p/README.md) |
| Guardian Project tor-android and Tor | Onion download streams | Exact prebuilt hashes, source revisions and preserved upstream notices in [native/tor](native/tor/README.md) |
| Quinn, rustls, rcgen and ring | Direct encrypted QUIC over the retained UDP socket | Locked dependency inventory in [native/direct](native/direct/README.md) |
| Beetswap and rust-libp2p | Explicitly paired IPFS Bitswap downloads | Locked dependencies and the documented authorization patch in [native/ipfs](native/ipfs/README.md) |

Each runtime's build tool collects its dependency license texts and provenance.
The patch bundle and patched APK include them under
`bitchord/runtimes/licenses/`, alongside the input hashes. The I2P bootstrap
certificates are copied from its pinned upstream source without alteration.
The existing Rust standard-library notices apply to all embedded Rust outputs.
