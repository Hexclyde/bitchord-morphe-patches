The experimental Soulseek JNI library is built from this repository's
`native/soulseek` adapter and the vendored engine at
`d20e433a8979068acd80f4d4242125beaf4825ee`, with local embedded-mode,
resource-bound and Android compatibility changes recorded alongside its source.
Its Rust dependency graph is locked in `native/soulseek/Cargo.lock`; the
[dependency and Rust standard-library notices](licenses/soulseek-native-dependencies.txt)
are also copied into the patched APK.
The inventory covers the full lock graph, including build-time and non-Android
dependencies, and is generated by `tools/native_licenses.py`. It preserves the
Rust standard-library notice appendix and includes pinned upstream license texts
where published crates omit their workspace's license files.

SeekerAndroid and StreamSeek were inspected as design references; their apps,
.NET/Node runtimes and source code are not bundled in the Soulseek extension.

`bitchord/runtimes/licenses/`, alongside the input hashes. The I2P bootstrap
certificates are copied from its pinned upstream source without alteration.
The existing Rust standard-library notices apply to all embedded Rust outputs.

## Fetched or used at run time, not bundled

- **Headphone correction** downloads headphone profiles from [AutoEq](https://github.com/jaakkopasanen/AutoEq) by Jaakko Pasanen ([MIT License](https://github.com/jaakkopasanen/AutoEq/blob/master/LICENSE)) when you pick a model, straight from its GitHub repository, and keeps them on the phone. The filter formulas are the peaking and shelving biquads of Robert Bristow-Johnson's Audio EQ Cookbook.
- The **JamesDSP** patch works with [RootlessJamesDSP](https://github.com/timschneeb/RootlessJamesDSP) by Tim Schneeberger and [JamesDSPManager](https://github.com/james34602/JamesDSPManager) by James Fung (both GPL-3.0), installed separately by you. Its setup help links to [Shizuku](https://github.com/RikkaApps/Shizuku) by RikkaApps (Apache-2.0), also installed separately, and its power switch is RootlessJamesDSP's own `SET_POWER_STATE` broadcast. Nothing of theirs is bundled; BitChord's audio session is announced to them through Android's standard audio-effect broadcasts.

## Flac Downloader Audio protocol reference

The independent BitChord adapter is based on the service interface observed in aztecempire/spotube-plugin-flac-audio, version 2.1.0, commit 4ecc535d57c32fe347a301e8fc54ce9be0430158. No plugin code, Hetu runtime or logo is included. The upstream README states MIT; the inspected tree contains no licence file. See [adapter documentation](docs/FLAC-AUDIO.md) for provenance and behavior.
